# Open Letter to Governor Greg Gianforte

**To:** Governor Greg Gianforte, State of Montana
**From:** Dwane Kimbrough
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** A housing model for Montana's AI workforce

Dear Governor Gianforte,

California attracted a generation of top engineers, designers, and AI specialists. It is now overcrowded, expensive, and politically strained. Many of these professionals are looking for an alternative.

Montana has what they need: space, natural cooling, and a climate that turns waste heat into a resource.

I am sharing a concept for a residential building that heats itself from the waste heat of an embedded AI compute cluster. The building produces its own food, collects its own water, generates its own power, and sells surplus compute to external clients. It is designed for cold climates, for seismic zones, and for people who work with their minds.

The full concept, including engineering nodes, legal analysis, and financial estimates, is publicly available:

https://github.com/dwfirst/simbiosis_homes

The repository was first published in November 2026. Commit history provides a dated record of the work. The documentation is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: attribution required, commercial use requires my permission.

I am not asking for funding. I am asking for feedback, and for a conversation about whether this model fits Montana's goals for economic development and rural resilience.

Montana has the land, the climate, and the need. The question is whether the state wants to attract the next wave of AI professionals on its own terms.

Respectfully,

Dwane Kimbrough
